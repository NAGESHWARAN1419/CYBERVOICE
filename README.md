# CYBERVOICE
A beginner Python-based cybersecurity awareness prototype that identifies suspicious keywords in user-provided messages and displays relevant safety advice.

**Voice-Based Cybersecurity Awareness Assistant**
Many smartphone users in India, especially students, elderly people, and small business owners, face difficulties identifying phishing messages, suspicious links, and online scams received through WhatsApp, SMS, and other digital platforms. These threats can occur during everyday activities such as online payments, shopping, and communication.
I propose a Voice-Based Cybersecurity Awareness Assistant that allows users to ask security-related questions and describe suspicious messages in simple Tamil or English. Using AI, the assistant will analyze the information, identify potential warning signs, and explain the risks in simple language. It will also provide practical safety recommendations to help users avoid online scams.
Speaking will be the main way to interact with the assistant because many users may find typing technical questions or understanding complex cybersecurity explanations difficult. A voice-first interface can make cybersecurity awareness more accessible, especially for people who are more comfortable speaking in their local language.
My goal is to create an easy-to-use assistant that helps people understand online threats and make safer digital decisions.
